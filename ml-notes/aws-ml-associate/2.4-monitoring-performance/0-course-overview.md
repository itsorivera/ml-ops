# Advanced Model Performance Analysis & Monitoring on AWS

This module focuses on the critical post-training phase of the ML lifecycle: analyzing, debugging, and monitoring model performance. It is designed for experienced practitioners seeking to master advanced techniques within the AWS ecosystem.

---

## Core Competencies

- **Performance Benchmarking:**
  - Establishing robust performance baselines for model evaluation.
  - Analyzing the trade-offs between predictive accuracy, training time, and infrastructure cost.

- **Advanced Model Evaluation:**
  - In-depth review of evaluation techniques and metrics for both classification and regression problems.

- **Training Optimization & Debugging:**
  - Diagnosing and resolving common training issues, with a focus on model convergence problems.
  - Implementing strategies for reproducible and consistent experimental results.

- **Model Interpretability & Explainability:**
  - Gaining deep insights into training data characteristics and potential biases.
  - Interpreting model predictions to understand and validate model behavior.

---

## Key AWS Services & Applications

- **Amazon SageMaker Clarify:**
  - Employ for data bias detection and generating model explainability reports (e.g., SHAP).

- **Amazon SageMaker Debugger:**
  - Utilize for real-time and post-hoc analysis of training jobs to debug convergence issues (e.g., vanishing/exploding gradients).

- **Amazon SageMaker Training Compiler & Automatic Model Tuning (AMT):**
  - Leverage to accelerate training and optimize hyperparameters, which can proactively address performance and convergence challenges.

By the end of this module, you will be equipped to systematically evaluate, debug, and optimize your ML models on AWS, ensuring they are not only performant but also robust, interpretable, and production-ready.
